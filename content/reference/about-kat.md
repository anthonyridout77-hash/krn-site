## About Kat

Nutritionist and Dietitian Brisbane

## Looking for a dietitian specialising in pregnancy related issues?

So, you’ve just found out the amazing news that you’re growing a little human. Congratulations, mama. What a special time this is for you and your family.
Maybe you’ve been dreaming about this moment for a while, or maybe it’s all brand new and a little overwhelming. Either way, it’s completely normal to feel that huge sense of responsibility and want to do everything right for your baby’s growth and wellbeing.
I want you to know you’re not alone. Every mum-to-be feels this mix of excitement, nerves, joy and “what now?”. As both a mum myself and an accredited dietitian, I understand exactly how those feelings show up in real life. I also know how much difference the right nutrition, guidance and support can make for you and your little one.
By being here, you’ve already taken a beautiful first step towards giving yourself and your baby the best possible care, now and into the future.

Even better, you’ve come to the right place for support and guidance on how to care for yourself and your baby with evidence-based, trustworthy advice. I’ve spent more than 12 years in university and postgraduate study, including 8 years in nutrition and 4 years specialising in prenatal nutrition. The good news is that you don’t have to sift through all the research or second-guess what’s best as I’ve already done the hard work for you! What I share is the most up-to-date, reliable and practical nutrition advice you can count on.
Pregnancy is such a unique window of time. What you eat now can shape not only your own health, but also your baby’s health, and even the health of your baby’s future children. Yes, you read that right. Pregnancy is an incredible opportunity to reset your health, nurture your family and create a strong foundation for generations to come!

### Kat’s Journey
I’ve always had a passion for health and fitness. I first completed a Bachelor of Business Management in Marketing at the University of Queensland while also working as a fitness and aerobics instructor in my spare time. A little further down the track, I realised I wanted to take that passion and use it to truly help people improve their health. That led me to four more years of study and a Bachelor of Applied Science (Honours) in Nutrition and Dietetics at the Queensland University of Technology.
Over the past ten years, I’ve worked as a dietitian in private practice and research roles. What I found myself most drawn to was supporting women through pregnancy and motherhood. I’ve had the privilege of walking alongside women at every stage of their journey. Whether they were planning a pregnancy, carrying their baby, or adjusting to life as a new mum; my focus has always been to equip them with the knowledge, skills and confidence to nourish themselves and their babies, creating the healthiest possible start for the next generation.
Together, we’ve tackled concerns surrounding:

- how to best nourish your body to prepare for pregnancy and labour, support your growing bub and recover post birth
- how to best manage, pregnancy related symptoms such as nausea, heartburn and constipation
- danger foods to avoid and nutrients to increase
- how to safely achieve a healthy weight before, during, and post pregnancy
- how to boost your milk supply
- how to optimise your little one’s growth and developmentAfter receiving several prestigious research scholarships throughout my studies and having three beautiful children of my own, I completed a post graduate degree in research to upskill specifically in the area I am most passionate about prenatal nutrition. 
I am privileged and honoured to share my knowledge and wealth of experience with women at all stages throughout the pregnancy lifecycle. I am here to support YOU!

## Help me help you!

Whether you are planning to have a baby, are already pregnant or you’ve had a baby and need some support in better managing your health during this pivotal and exciting time, I can help.
I have experience in treating a range of general clinical conditions and have an extensive understanding of diet-disease relationships. I have a particular interest in pregnancy-related nutrition and health (including the pre-conception, prenatal and postnatal periods) and have recently received APD certification in advanced paediatric nutrition. I am therefore equipped to help mums to be, pregnant women and new mums and bubs who may require support in managing their health.

I like to promote an education and outcomes-based approach, and I make sure patients are assessed and evaluated regularly to review if programs are working for them. I provide patients with education, tools and practical skills (individualised meal planning/goal setting/mindfulness strategies/ label reading etc) to meet their nutritional requirements while fitting into their lifestyle and family needs.
Importantly, I authentically care for the health and well-being of my patients and build a positive relationship based on trust, respect, empathy, compassion, and encouragement with the people in my care.
