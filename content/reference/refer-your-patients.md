## Refer Your Patients

As an APD, I can assist all people who wish to achieve optimal health status, providing education, strategies and motivation to address health concerns such as diabetes, cardiovascular disease, weight management, disordered eating, gut and metabolic conditions, food allergy and intolerance, as well as general lifestyle and healthy eating advice, however, my main areas of interest are surrounding prenatal nutrition.
