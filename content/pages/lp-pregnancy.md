Common questions

Do I need a referral to see you?

No referral needed. You can book directly online.

Can you help with gestational diabetes?

Yes. GDM is one of my specialties. I help women manage blood sugar through practical, real-food nutrition planning that works around pregnancy cravings and nausea.

What if I have morning sickness and can barely eat?

This is very common and something I work around constantly. I have practical strategies for getting essential nutrients even when appetite is limited or unreliable.

Is nutrition support safe during pregnancy?

Completely. All recommendations are evidence-based and aligned with Australian pregnancy nutrition guidelines. I work alongside your midwife or obstetrician.

Are Medicare rebates available?

Rebates may apply with a GP referral for specific pregnancy conditions like gestational diabetes. Ask me on the call and I will clarify what applies to your situation.
