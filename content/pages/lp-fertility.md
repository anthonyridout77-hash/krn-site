Common questions

Do I need a referral to see you?

No referral needed. You can book directly online.

Can you help with PCOS, endometriosis or unexplained infertility?

Yes. I specialise in all three and take an evidence-based approach tailored to your specific diagnosis through targeted nutrition support that works alongside their medical team.

Do you work with women going through IVF?

Absolutely. Nutrition during IVF can support your overall health and wellbeing through treatment, and may help optimise the conditions for a successful cycle. I work alongside your fertility specialist to complement your protocol.

How soon could I start seeing results?

Timelines vary a lot from person to person, but many women notice changes to hormonal markers and cycle regularity within a few months of consistent nutrition changes. Egg quality takes longer to influence — generally at least one full ovulation cycle, around 3 months — so it’s worth starting early and staying consistent.

Are Medicare rebates available?

Rebates may apply if you have a chronic disease management plan from your GP. Ask me on the call and I will clarify what applies to your situation.
