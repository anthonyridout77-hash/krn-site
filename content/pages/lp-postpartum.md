Common questions

Do I need a referral to see you?

No referral needed. You can book directly online.

How soon after birth can I start?

Any time. Whether you are days or months postpartum, nutrition support is relevant and beneficial at any stage of recovery.

Can nutrition help with breastfeeding?

Yes. I provide specific guidance on nutrient needs while breastfeeding, milk supply support, and managing the physical demands of feeding a baby.

I have no time and no energy. Will this actually fit my life?

That is the first thing I address. Everything I recommend is designed around what is realistic for a new parent — no complicated meal prep required.

Are Medicare rebates available?

Rebates may apply if you have a chronic disease management plan from your GP. Ask me on the call and I will clarify what applies to your situation.
