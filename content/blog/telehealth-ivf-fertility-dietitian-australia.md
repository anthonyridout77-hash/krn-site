## Why telehealth works for fertility nutrition

* Most of the work is conversation, planning and follow-up, with no physical exam needed
* Blood results and clinic letters can be shared electronically
* No travel between clinic appointments during an IVF cycle
* Regional clients can see a fertility-focused APD without leaving home
* Couples can join from different locations

## How IVF nutrition support works

1. **Before your cycle:** a full review of history, bloods, medications and diet for both partners
2. **Stimulation and retrieval:** practical eating around injections, bloating and fatigue
3. **Transfer and the two-week wait:** steady, simple eating and supplement checks
4. **After a result:** pregnancy nutrition, or support and planning for the next cycle

The IVF Preparation Program (7 consults) is built around this timeline. More: [IVF dietitian support](https://www.katridout.com.au/blogs/ivf-dietitian-brisbane-nutrition/).

## FAQ

**Can I claim Medicare for a telehealth dietitian consult?**  
Yes, if you’re eligible under a GP Chronic Condition Management Plan: a Medicare rebate per session for up to 5 sessions a year. Your GP decides eligibility.

**Does diet affect IVF outcomes?**  
Research links Mediterranean-style eating and adequate folate, vitamin D and omega-3s with better fertility outcomes, though no diet guarantees a result.

**Will the dietitian talk to my fertility clinic?**  
With your consent, notes can be shared with your GP or fertility specialist.
