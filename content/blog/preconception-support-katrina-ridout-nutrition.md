**Quick answer:** Yes. Katrina Ridout Nutrition offers preconception nutrition support for individuals and couples, led by Katrina Ridout, an Accredited Practising Dietitian (APD). Options range from a single initial consult to multi-consult programs such as The Program and the IVF Preparation Program. Consults run in person in Milton, Brisbane, or by telehealth Australia-wide.

*By Katrina Ridout, Accredited Practising Dietitian (APD), Brisbane. Reviewed October 2026.*

![Balanced preconception meal](/images/wp/2026/07/krn-preconception-nutrition-body1.jpg)

## Who preconception support is for

* Anyone planning a pregnancy in the next 3–12 months
* Couples who want both partners’ diets reviewed
* People with PMOS (previously called PCOS), endometriosis, thyroid conditions or irregular cycles
* People preparing for IVF or other fertility treatment
* Anyone after a pregnancy loss who wants support before trying again

## What’s included

* A 75-minute initial consult reviewing your history, cycle, bloods and current eating
* A personalised supplement plan at the first consult (folic acid, iodine and anything your results show you need)
* A written plan and resources sent within 48 hours
* Reviews to adjust the plan and keep you on track
* Notes shared with your GP or fertility specialist when you’d like

Start with the basics in [what to eat before trying to conceive](https://www.katridout.com.au/blogs/what-to-eat-before-trying-to-conceive/).

## Choosing an option

* **Single consult**: a plan and supplement review to get started
* **The Program** (initial + 4 reviews): covers the 3-month preconception window
* **The Journey** (initial + 7 reviews): support that continues into pregnancy and postpartum
* **IVF Preparation Program** (7 consults): timed around your treatment cycle
* **Couples Fertility Program**: both partners together

Program consults are flexible and can be used across preconception, pregnancy and postpartum.

**Where:** in person at Milton Village Medical, Level 1, 36 Baroona Road, Milton QLD 4064, or by telehealth anywhere in Australia. Phone 1300 640 104.

## Rebates and next steps

Medicare rebates may be available if your GP has set up a GP Chronic Condition Management Plan (GPCCMP), and many private health funds cover dietitian consults under extras. A good first step is a free 15-minute call with Kat to talk through what is going on and which option suits you.

## FAQ

**How far ahead should I start preconception nutrition?**  
Around 3 months before trying is ideal, because eggs and sperm take about that long to mature.

**Can my partner come too?**  
Yes. Couples initial consults are 90 minutes, and there’s a Couples Fertility Program and a partner add-on.

**Do I need a GP referral?**  
No, unless you want to claim Medicare rebates under a GP Chronic Condition Management Plan.
