**Quick answer:** In Australia, an **Accredited Practising Dietitian (APD)** is university-trained in clinical nutrition. APDs can assess and treat medical conditions such as gestational diabetes, anaemia, PMOS (previously called PCOS) and severe nausea, and are recognised by Medicare and private health funds. **“Nutritionist” is not a protected title.** A nutritionist may give general healthy-eating advice, but they can’t claim Medicare, and their training varies widely. For pregnancy, especially with a medical condition, see an APD.

*By Katrina Ridout, Accredited Practising Dietitian (APD), Brisbane. Reviewed September 2026.*

## Side-by-side

|  | Accredited Practising Dietitian (APD) | Nutritionist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Training | Accredited university dietetics degree plus supervised clinical placements | Varies, from a science degree to a short course |
| Protected title | Yes (APD credential) | No |
| Ongoing CPD required | Yes, via Dietitians Australia | Only if registered with a voluntary body |
| Treats medical conditions | Yes (medical nutrition therapy) | No |
| Medicare (GPCCMP) rebates | Yes, for eligible patients | No |
| Private health extras | Yes | Rarely |

## Why it matters more in pregnancy

Pregnancy changes your nutrient needs and brings conditions that need clinical management:

* **Gestational diabetes:** carbohydrate distribution, glucose targets and working with your diabetes educator. See [just diagnosed with gestational diabetes](https://www.katridout.com.au/blogs/gestational-diabetes-just-diagnosed-what-now/).
* **Iron deficiency:** food, supplement choice and absorption. See [iron in pregnancy](https://www.katridout.com.au/blogs/iron-in-pregnancy-tired-before-anaemia/).
* **Food safety:** listeria, mercury and vitamin A limits.
* **Pre-existing conditions:** PMOS, endometriosis, coeliac disease, eating concerns or a history of bariatric surgery.

An APD works alongside your GP, obstetrician and midwife, and can write back to them.

## When a nutritionist might be enough

For general healthy-eating ideas with no medical history, a degree-qualified nutritionist (for example, a Registered Nutritionist with the Nutrition Society of Australia) can be helpful. Once there’s a diagnosis, a medication or an IVF cycle involved, choose an APD.

## FAQ

**Can a nutritionist help with gestational diabetes?**  
Gestational diabetes is a medical condition. In Australia it should be managed by an APD alongside your diabetes team.

**Is a dietitian also a nutritionist?**  
Yes. Every APD is qualified as a nutritionist, but the reverse isn’t true.

**Does Medicare cover a pregnancy dietitian?**  
Eligible patients on a GP Chronic Condition Management Plan can claim a rebate for up to 5 allied health visits a year with an APD. Your GP decides eligibility.
