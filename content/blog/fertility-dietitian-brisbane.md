**Quick answer:** A fertility dietitian is an Accredited Practising Dietitian (APD) who specialises in preconception, IVF and conditions that affect fertility such as PMOS (previously called PCOS), endometriosis and male factor infertility. In Brisbane, Katrina Ridout Nutrition sees clients in person at Milton Village Medical (Milton QLD 4064) and by telehealth, and supports couples, not just the person trying to conceive.

*By Katrina Ridout, Accredited Practising Dietitian (APD), Brisbane. Reviewed October 2026.*

## What to look for in a Brisbane fertility dietitian

* **APD credential**, checkable on the Dietitians Australia directory
* **A fertility-focused caseload**: preconception, IVF, PMOS, endometriosis, recurrent miscarriage support, male fertility
* **Assessment before advice**: bloods, cycle, medications and medical history
* **Works with your clinic**: shares notes with your GP or fertility specialist
* **Partner support**: sperm take around 3 months to develop, so both partners’ diets matter
* **No promises**: nobody can guarantee a pregnancy

More detail: [how to choose a fertility dietitian](https://www.katridout.com.au/blogs/how-to-choose-a-fertility-dietitian/) and [what a fertility dietitian does](https://www.katridout.com.au/blogs/what-does-a-fertility-dietitian-do/).

## FAQ

**How long before trying to conceive should I see a fertility dietitian?**  
Ideally 3 months or more, as eggs and sperm take around 3 months to mature. Starting later still helps.

**Can a dietitian help if we’re doing IVF?**  
Yes. A dietitian can help you prepare before a cycle, manage side effects and support your partner. They work alongside your fertility specialist, not instead of them.

**Is there a Medicare rebate?**  
Eligible patients on a GP Chronic Condition Management Plan can claim a Medicare rebate per session for up to 5 sessions a year. Your GP decides eligibility.
