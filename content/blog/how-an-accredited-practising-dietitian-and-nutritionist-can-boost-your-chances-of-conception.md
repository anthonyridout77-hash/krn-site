## Key Ways Nutrition Affects Fertility

* **Balanced Hormones**

Hormonal imbalances are a common cause of fertility issues, especially in women. Nutrients like zinc, magnesium, and vitamin D are essential for regulating hormones involved in reproduction. A diet rich in these can help balance your hormones and support ovulation.

* **Weight Management**

Being overweight or underweight can affect fertility. Excess body fat can disrupt hormonal balance, while being underweight can lead to a lack of ovulation. A dietitian and nutritionist will help you reach and maintain a healthy weight through balanced nutrition and proper portion control.

* **Antioxidants and Fertility**

Antioxidants, such as vitamins C and E, protect eggs and sperm from oxidative stress, which can negatively affect their quality. A diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and whole grains can provide the necessary antioxidants to support fertility.

* **Gut Health**

Your gut health is closely tied to overall well-being, including fertility. Dietitians and nutritionists can help you optimise your gut health by recommending foods that improve digestion and reduce inflammation, both of which can support reproductive health.
