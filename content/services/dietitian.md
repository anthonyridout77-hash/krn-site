## What a dietitian does

Dietitians are allied health professionals with university qualifications, trained to assess and manage a wide range of health conditions through food. They have the medical knowledge and practical experience to guide you through everyday nutrition as well as more complex conditions that need dietary support, and the advice they give is evidence based and sustainable.

### What is an Accredited Practising Dietitian?

An Accredited Practising Dietitian (APD) holds the national credential for dietitians in Australia, which requires university training in clinical nutrition and ongoing professional development. It is also the credential Medicare and private health funds recognise.
