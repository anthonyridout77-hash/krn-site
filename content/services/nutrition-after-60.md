Katrina Ridout, Accredited Practising Dietitian, offers individual dietetic assessment and practical nutrition support for men and women in their 60s and 70s, covering strength and muscle, weight changes, cholesterol, blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, bone health, appetite and everyday eating. Appointments are in person at Milton Village Medical, Brisbane, or by telehealth across Australia. Kat provides nutrition assessment and advice; diagnosis and medicines stay with your GP or specialist. Start with a free 15-minute phone call.
