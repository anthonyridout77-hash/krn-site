## A healthy weight supports your baby’s best start — and it’s never too late to begin

Achieving a healthy weight can feel challenging at the best of times, especially when emotions and hormones are changing in the lead-up to and during pregnancy. The good news is that these very changes are also what help your body to conceive and carry a baby.
By supporting your body with balanced nutrition and healthy habits, you can improve your chances of conceiving and set yourself up for a smoother, healthier pregnancy.

## Calculate your BMI:

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a tool used to guide how much weight gain is recommended during pregnancy.

You can check your BMI using the calculator below.

## BMI categories

- Below 18.5: Underweight
- 18.5–24.9: Healthy weight range
- 25.0–29.9: Overweight
- 30.0 and above: Obese
### Factors that can influence how much weight you gain

- The types of foods you eat and how you eat them
- Your activity levels during pregnancy
- Pregnancy-related symptoms such as nausea and fatigue
- Changes in appetite, including food cravings and aversions
- Fluid retention
### Quick facts:

#### The major nutrients you should incorporate into your diet during pregnancy are:

- Proteins (cooked meat, chicken, fish, beans, dairy products, soy products)
- Folate (legumes, leafy greens, asparagus, eggs, beets, citrus)
- Iron (poultry, fish, eggs, beans, leafy greens, nuts, seeds, wholegrains)
- Iodine (grains, dairy products, cooked fish, seaweed, fruits and veggies)
- Vitamin D (fatty fish, soy milk, orange juice, cheese, eggs)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is food safety so important during pregnancy?

During pregnancy, your immune system naturally lowers to help protect your growing baby. This makes you more vulnerable to foodborne illnesses such as listeria and salmonella. Being mindful with food choices and preparation helps protect both you and your baby.

### What foods should I avoid while pregnant?

It’s best to avoid foods with a higher risk of contamination, such as raw or undercooked meat and eggs, soft cheeses, chilled seafood, deli meats, unpasteurised milk or juice, and pre-prepared salads. I’ll guide you with safe alternatives so you never feel restricted.

### Can nutrition really strengthen my immune system during pregnancy?

Yes. While your immune system naturally changes in pregnancy, the right nutrition can help keep it strong. Eating a balanced diet rich in vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, lean proteins and healthy fats can support both your immunity and your baby’s growth.
