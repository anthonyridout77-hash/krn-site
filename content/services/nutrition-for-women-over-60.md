Katrina Ridout, Accredited Practising Dietitian, offers individual dietetic assessment and practical nutrition support for women over 60, covering changes in appetite, strength and muscle, bone health, cholesterol, type 2 diabetes and everyday eating. Appointments are in person at Milton Village Medical, Brisbane, or by telehealth across Australia. Kat provides nutrition assessment and advice; diagnosis and medicines stay with your GP or specialist. Start with a free 15-minute phone call.
