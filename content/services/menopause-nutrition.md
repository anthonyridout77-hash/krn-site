Katrina Ridout, Accredited Practising Dietitian, offers individual dietetic assessment and practical nutrition support for people in perimenopause, menopause and the years after. Appointments are in person at Milton Village Medical, Brisbane, or by telehealth across Australia. Kat provides nutrition assessment and advice; diagnosis and decisions about medicines, including menopausal hormone therapy, stay with your GP or specialist. Start with a free 15-minute phone call.
