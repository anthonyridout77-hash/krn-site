Status 6 Nov 2026: all six scenarios are inactive and `scenarios_run` refuses inactive scenarios ("Scenario is not activated"), so no end-to-end run was possible. Everything below is the plan plus what was verified without running: blueprints were accepted by Make (`scenarios_update` succeeded for 8408756, 8408758, 8408761, 8408776, 8408782), the holidays store holds 29 records, and `npm run build` passes with both pages excluded from the sitemap and `noindex`.
